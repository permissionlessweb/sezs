The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "MAY", "RECOMMENDED",
"OPTIONAL", and "REQUIRED" in this document, and in every other SEZS
specification, are to be interpreted as described in
[BCP 14](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/bcp14)
([RFC 2119](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119),
[RFC 8174](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174)) when, and only when, they
appear in all capitals.

Document structure follows [ZIP-0000](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000)
(fixed preamble, section order, and a clear split between requirements and
the specification body). [ZIP-0001](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0001) is cited
here as part of the same published ZIP series; it is Zcash network-upgrade
policy and scheduling, which SEZS does not adopt.

Later specifications MUST NOT repeat this keyword paragraph. Each of them MUST
adopt it with a single sentence that points here and to BCP 14, then include a
Definitions section as specified below.

## Definitions

- **SEZS specification.** A design document that describes a component of
  Speak-Ez Sessions, or a process used to produce those documents. It is not a
  Zcash ZIP. Identifiers use the `SEZS-NNNN` form.

## Abstract

This document defines how SEZS specifications are written, numbered, and revised.
It copies the useful parts of [ZIP-0000](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000) — a fixed
preamble, a fixed section order, BCP 14 language, and a clear split between
requirements and the specification body — and drops the parts that only make
sense inside the Zcash ZIP editorship (network-upgrade deployment, ZIP editors,
Zcash Community Forum workflow).

The intended outcome is a single, reviewable set of documents in `docs/specs/`
that both a grant reviewer and an implementer can use without reading informal
notes scattered through the tree.

## Motivation

The repository already contained honest but informal notes (`TICKET-V2-SPEC`,
circuit and manifold `SPEC.md` files, identity and email gap documents, usher
Bluetooth notes). Those notes overlapped, aged at different rates, and did not
share a structure that made requirements checkable. A common format makes it
possible to point a Quint model or a Rust trait at a numbered requirement and
say whether the implementation meets it.

## Privacy Implications

This process document does not change any protocol. Individual specifications
MUST include a Privacy Implications section when the component stores, transmits,
or omits personal or ticket-secret data.

## Requirements

- Every specification MUST start with the RFC 822-style preamble defined below.
- Every specification MUST remain complete without its Abstract.
- After the preamble, every specification other than this one MUST include a
  single sentence adopting the keyword and document conventions of this ZIP
  (BCP 14 and the ZIP conventions recorded here). It MUST NOT restate the
  keyword paragraph.
- Every specification MUST include a Definitions section after that convention
  sentence (or, in this process document, after the convention text) and before
  the Abstract. Definitions MUST list terms defined by that document. Terms
  defined in another SEZS specification MUST be linked to that document rather
  than restated.
- Requirements sections MUST NOT themselves contain conformance language that
  belongs in the Specification. The Specification MUST be detailed enough that
  a second implementation could interoperate.
- Unknowns MUST be listed as Open Issues. Authors MUST NOT invent behaviour
  to close a gap.
- Public specifications MUST NOT describe laboratory payment rails or include
  real registration keys, secrets, or machine identifiers.
- Every cross-reference to another SEZS specification MUST be a relative
  Markdown hyperlink to that document's filename.

## Specification

### Numbering and status

Numbers are assigned in `docs/specs/` and recorded in [`docs/specs/README.md`](README.md).
A number MAY be reserved with a stub whose Status is `Reserved`. Once published
as `Draft`, the number is not reused for a different topic.

Status values: `Reserved`, `Draft`, `Proposed`, `Active`, `Implemented`,
`Withdrawn`, `Obsolete`.

Categories MAY be one or more of: `Process`, `Circuit`, `Contract`,
`Interface`, `Protocol`. Multiple categories are separated by ` / `.

### Preamble

Required headers: `ZIP` (the `SEZS-NNNN` identifier), `Title`, `Owners`,
`Status`, `Category`, `Created` (`yyyy-mm-dd`), `License`.

Optional headers: `Credits`, `Original-Authors`, `Discussions-To`,
`Pull-Request`, `Last-Updated`, `Obsoleted-By`, `Updated-By`, `Obsoletes`,
`Updates`.

License for new SEZS specifications MUST be Apache-2.0, matching the public
repository. Documents are written in Markdown.

### Section order

Preamble; a single sentence adopting this document's keyword and ZIP
conventions (this process document includes the full convention text
instead of that sentence); Definitions; Abstract; Motivation; Privacy
Implications (when applicable); Requirements; Specification; Rationale;
Reference Implementation; Open Issues; optional Change History.

The Specification is the only section that states conformance rules for
implementations. Formal models live in `docs/specs/quint/` and are cited from
the relevant specification.

### Relationship to code

A specification MAY lag or lead the code. When they disagree, the Open Issues
section MUST say so. The Reference Implementation section MUST point at tree
paths or public hyperlinks, not at a private checkout.

## Rationale

[ZIP-0000](https://zips.z.cash/zip-0000) is a widely read format for
privacy-preserving money protocols. Using its structure lets reviewers apply a
familiar checklist without implying that SEZS is a Zcash consensus change.
Keeping informal notes as stubs avoids a second, drifting source of truth.
Centralising keyword rules here keeps later documents readable.

## Reference Implementation

This process is implemented by the documents under `docs/specs/` and the
`just specs-quint` recipe in the repository justfile. The public series is
published from this tree on the docs site, which derives pages from these
files at build time.

## Open Issues

- There is no separate editorship or public comment forum for SEZS
  specifications yet. Until one exists, Owners are the SEZS contributors
  listed in the preamble, and discussion happens in the
  [permissionlessweb/sezs](https://github.com/permissionlessweb/sezs)
  repository.
