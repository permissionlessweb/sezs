# Disclaimers

Please read this before you buy a ticket, send a payment, or rely on this software.

## Ticket sales

Ticket sales are final, unless the venue is at capacity when a ticket holder arrives at the door. In that case the holder receives a full refund.

## Software

The software has not been audited. The circuits and contracts are unreviewed. This is pilot software.

There is no warranty, express or implied, including for merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## Keys and tickets

You are responsible for your keys and for any ticket secret you hold. The ticket secret is derived on your device and is never sent to the backend. If you lose the secret and have no backup, the ticket cannot be recovered by the organizers' servers.

## Privacy limits

Be honest with yourself about what this system hides and what it does not.

A 15–300 person event has a small anonymity set. A proof that you hold a valid ticket does not make you indistinguishable in a crowd that small.

The backend sees payment memos. For a v2 ticket the memo is the commitment itself. The backend does not learn the ticket secret, but it can see that a payment arrived with that memo.

Invite links, hash fragments, and shielded payments reduce casual leakage. They are not a guarantee against the operator, the chain, or a determined observer.
