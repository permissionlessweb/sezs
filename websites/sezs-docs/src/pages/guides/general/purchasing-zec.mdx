# Buy ZEC for the first time

This page is general information, not an offer to sell ZEC and not a recommendation of any particular venue. Rules differ by country and sometimes by region. Check the law that applies to you, including whether you must use a venue that verifies identity, before you buy.

## A few common paths

People usually obtain ZEC in one of these ways:

- A regulated exchange or broker in their jurisdiction that lists ZEC, if one is available to them.
- A peer-to-peer sale or another over-the-counter route, where that is lawful.
- ZEC they already hold in another wallet, including ZEC received as a gift or a wage.

The names above are categories, not endorsements. Fees, liquidity, withdrawal rules, and privacy practices vary. Prefer a venue you can verify independently, and be wary of anyone who contacts you first offering a "better rate."

## Move it to a wallet you control

If you buy ZEC on a venue, withdraw it to a receive address in [Zafu](https://zafu.pro) (or another wallet whose keys you hold). Ticket invoices are paid from a wallet you control, with a memo you attach. Leaving the funds on an exchange is not the same as having them ready for a shielded payment.

Confirm the address on the device before you withdraw. Send a small test amount first if you are new to the wallet.

## Privacy and records

A purchase venue may collect identity and may see the withdrawal address. Once the funds are in your wallet, later payments still have limits: the event backend sees payment memos, and a small event has a small anonymity set. See [Disclaimers](/disclaimers).
