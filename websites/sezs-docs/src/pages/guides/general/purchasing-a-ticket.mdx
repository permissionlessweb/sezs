# Buy a ticket

Read the [disclaimers](/disclaimers) before you pay. Ticket sales are final, unless the venue is at capacity when you arrive at the door, in which case you receive a full refund. This software has not been audited.

Buy only from the official event site, [permissionless.money/alpha/sezs](https://permissionless.money/alpha/sezs/). Do not pay an invoice from a forwarded screenshot alone.

## Before you start

- A ZEC wallet you control, preferably [Zafu](https://zafu.pro), with enough ZEC to cover the tier plus network fees. See [Buy ZEC for the first time](/guides/general/purchasing-zec) if you do not hold any yet.

## Pay the invoice

On the event page, choose a tier. The page derives a ticket secret on your device and shows a v2 invoice: a shielded ZEC address and a memo that is only the commitment (the `sezs1:` prefix plus hex). The invoice does not include a ticket public key or a nullifier.

Send the payment with that memo attached exactly. If the memo is missing or changed, the organizers may not be able to match the payment to your commitment.

The backend can see the memo. It does not learn the secret, and it must not be given the secret. That rule is part of the [ticketing spec](/specs/ticketing).

## Back up the ticket

When the page offers a backup, save it somewhere you control — printed, or stored offline. Anyone who has the secret can prove the ticket. If you lose the secret and have no backup, the backend cannot rebuild it for you.

You can move the backup to another device you control before the door. Treat it like a key.

## At the door

Show the ticket QR from this browser, from a passkey-unlocked copy, or from a paper printout of the same code. You do not build a proof on your phone. An event usher scans the code, their device builds the proof, and they authorize the consume with Zafu. A second scan is refused.

See [Check in at the door](/guides/general/check-in) for the payload, the trust model, and what the usher does.

## If something goes wrong

- If the venue is at capacity when you arrive with a valid ticket, you are owed a full refund, as stated in the [disclaimers](/disclaimers).
- If you paid without the memo, contact the organizers through the channel they publish, and do not send the secret in that message.
- If a page or a person asks you to paste the ticket secret to a server, stop.
